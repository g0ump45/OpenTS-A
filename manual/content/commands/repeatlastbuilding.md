---
command_id: RepeatLastBuilding
---

Starts another structure of the type that last finished construction for the player, as a click on its cameo would, when the [sidebar](/systems/sidebar/) still offers that type. It is refused, with the busy-factory voice line, while a structure is under construction or on hold, and silently while a finished structure waits to be placed. Nothing happens before the player has completed a structure in the current game.
