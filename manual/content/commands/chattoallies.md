---
command_id: ChatToAllies
---

Opens the message editor addressed to the sender's allies, or to the other observers when the sender is an observer, in a game against other machines and while no line is being edited. A defeated player given the whole map cannot open it; [observers and coach mode](/systems/observers/#coach-mode) says who that is. It takes Backspace when [`KEYBOARD.INI`](/formats/keyboard-ini/) binds neither the command nor the key. [In-game chat](/systems/chat/) says who receives the line.
