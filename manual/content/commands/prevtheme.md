---
command_id: PrevTheme
---

Plays the track before the one playing, wrapping from the first track to the last, and shows its name on screen for four seconds. A track the game does not allow at the time is skipped: one the campaign has not reached, one that belongs to the other side, or one that is not a battle track. With no track playing it starts from the last one. When no track is allowed, nothing happens.
