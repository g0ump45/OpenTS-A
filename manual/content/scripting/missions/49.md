---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_SUCCESS
title: Success
summary: Marks the team as having succeeded, which is read once the team is disbanded and nowhere else.
caveats:
  - The line changes nothing about the team, its members or its orders. It sets one mark and moves the team on to the next line the following turn.
  - The mark is never cleared, so reaching the line once is enough however long the script runs afterward and however the team ends.
  - Nothing else in the game raises that mark. A team that fights well and dies before reaching this line records a failure.
  - The outcome is recorded against every AI trigger that names the team's TeamType first, whatever raised the team, so a team the scenario placed by hand moves those records too.
related:
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-production
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
---

Disbanding the team is what consumes the mark, and [the AI trigger track record](/systems/ai-team-production/#the-track-record) owns what it is worth: which triggers are credited, how much the weight moves, and what the alternative costs. Nothing reads the mark while the team is alive.
