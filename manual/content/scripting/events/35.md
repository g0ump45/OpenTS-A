## What the scan takes

A beam runs the scan once per frame and springs the event under **all of:**

- the beam is sweeping its arc;
- the structure carrying it is still standing, [powered on](/systems/power/#fields-fences-and-lights), and holds a tag;
- a soldier or a vehicle standing in the beam's own cell or one of the eight around it is not an ally of the structure's house and lies within [thirty leptons more than the beam's detection radius](/keys/spotlightradius/) of the beam.

That radius grows as the beam swings away from the structure, so the same nine cells are searched throughout but the distance the intruder is allowed to stand at is not fixed.

## Which beams reach the scan

[The beam's behavior](/reference/enums/spotlight-behavior/) decides whether the scan is reached at all. A sweeping beam runs it every frame. A circling beam and a beam held still never do, so [switching a beam to either](/mapping/actions/taction-change-spotlight-behavior/) silences the event for as long as it stays there.

A following beam runs the scan on the one frame it loses its target and drops back to sweeping. On that frame the beam's position is written back from a figure the follow path never worked out, so where the scan looks is not settled by where the beam had been.
