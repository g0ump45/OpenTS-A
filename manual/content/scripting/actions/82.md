title: Wakeup all sleepers
summary: Puts every sleeping vehicle, infantryman and aircraft that the trigger's house does not own onto guard.
caveats:
  - The trigger's own house is the one house left asleep. Everything sleeping that belongs to anybody else is woken, allies included.
  - Only vehicles, infantry and aircraft are examined. A structure on the Sleep mission stays on it.
  - Destroyed objects and objects in limbo are passed over, so troops riding in a transport keep sleeping until they are set down.
