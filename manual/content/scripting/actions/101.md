---
type: action
id: TACTION_FLASH_TEAM
title: Flash Team...
summary: Flashes the members of every live team built from the team type the line names.
caveats:
  - The line names a team type rather than one team. Every team on the map built from that type flashes, and a team type with no live team leaves the action doing nothing at all.
  - Only the infantry, vehicles and aircraft a team holds are reached. A structure is never a team member and never flashes.
  - The count is stamped onto each member once. Anything the team recruits afterward does not flash, and anything that leaves the team keeps flashing out the count it was given.
  - The count is held as a number that cannot be negative. A negative duration therefore becomes an enormous one and the members flash for the rest of the mission.
related:
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_FLASH
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_TALK_BUBBLE
---
