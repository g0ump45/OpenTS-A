---
type: action
id: TACTION_DEACTIVATE_FIRESTORM
title: Deactivate Firestorm Defense
summary: Fires the trigger house's firestorm superweapon so that its firestorm walls come down.
caveats:
  - The wall is lowered by firing the weapon rather than by being switched off, so the action does nothing at all when the wall is already down.
  - The house's own copy of the first firestorm superweapon in the declared list is the one fired. A rules tree that declares none leaves the action with nothing to fire.
  - For a house under human control the drain still standing is converted back into charge, so the sooner the wall is taken down the sooner it can be raised again.
