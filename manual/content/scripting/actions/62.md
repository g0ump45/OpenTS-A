---
type: action
id: TACTION_TURN_ON_ATTACHED
title: Turn on building
summary: Puts every switched-off structure whose tag is attached to this trigger back into service.
caveats:
  - The action sweeps every structure in the scenario and switches on each one whose tag is attached to the same trigger; it is not limited to the structure that sprang it. A structure in limbo, or one not yet put down on the map, is passed over.
  - Only a structure that has been switched off is reached. A structure whose functions are cut back because its house is short of power has not been switched off, so the action passes over it and the shortfall is untouched.
