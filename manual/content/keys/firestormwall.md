Every structure of such a type that a house owns is switched together when its [firestorm superweapon](/systems/superweapons/#the-firestorm-defense) is fired, rather than one section at a time. [The firestorm wall](/systems/laser-fences/#the-firestorm-wall) covers what the flag does to placement, to the shape a section draws, to what a raised section destroys, and to what selling one returns.

The [power toggle](/systems/power/#switching-a-structure-off) refuses such a structure outright, so a wall section cannot be switched off to open a gap in the line.
