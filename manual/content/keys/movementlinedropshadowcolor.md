---
key: MovementLineDropShadowColor
summary: The colour of the shadow below the movement line, as red, green and blue from 0 to 255.
see_also: ["system:action-lines", MovementLineDropShadow, MovementLineColor]
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "0,0,0"
---

The three numbers are separated by commas; a value that is not three numbers keeps the default, black. The colour is used only with [`MovementLineDropShadow=yes`](/keys/movementlinedropshadow/).
