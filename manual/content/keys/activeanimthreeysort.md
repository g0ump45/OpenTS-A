The bias that moves the third slot's animation within the drawing order of the objects sharing its layer. [Placement and draw order](/systems/building-animations/#placement-and-draw-order) covers how that differs from the depth bias, what the slot does to the AnimType's own sort bias, and the byte the figure has to fit in.
