Modern Touch supports a quick, stationary two-finger tap for a right click at the first finger. Holding a production icon for half a second and releasing also right-clicks it, allowing production to be paused or canceled. The scheme menu lists Modern Touch above Original Touch, retaining the default and saved scheme values.

Selection drags now finish on release rather than entering attack-move after a long hold. Cancellation, loss of focus and switching to pinch clear the selection box.
