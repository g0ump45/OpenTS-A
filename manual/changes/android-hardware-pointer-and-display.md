Physical mice use game cursor artwork and the visible battlefield boundary for
edge scrolling. Arrow keys can be assigned in the shortcut picker. Common, native and custom display sizes apply on the next app start. Active
and saved sizes are shown separately. Menus scale separately from the battlefield at native and custom sizes. Saves
and key-file formats are unchanged.
